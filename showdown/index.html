        All the rules
        1. You choose every attack: pick your troop, then the enemy troop it hits.
        2. Damage beyond a troop's last orb hits its owner's Base. A troop that survives hits back.
        3. A Base can be struck directly only when its side has no troops.
        4. Play first, then act: one troop, one other card (spell, gear or location) and one trap a turn. A free troop rests on the turn it arrives; a tribute troop strikes at once.
        5. Instead of attacking, a troop can take cover: damage past its last orb stays off your Base, but it does not hit back.
        6. A trap waits face down and springs on the next enemy attack.
        7. Gear stays on one troop. One location is in play for the whole table; a new one replaces it.
        8. An empty deck costs your Base an orb each turn.
